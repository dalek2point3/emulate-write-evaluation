## Main findings

Pangram labeled most outputs Human, but writing quality was uneven. Science/technology had the highest mean reviewer score (4.30/5), while essays/humanities had the lowest (3.17/5). These are descriptive results on these prompts, not validated product-wide rankings.

13 outputs received coherence/readability scores of 1 or 2 out of 5, indicating major problems under the stated rubric. Several detector-Human examples illustrate why detection and quality must be reported separately:

- W013 repeatedly says one should usually break promises, then concludes one should usually keep them.
- W047 switches which team is visiting and calls an equalizer a break in the deadlock.
- W055 leaves “Thanks for Helping Me Move Sample” in the letter body.
- W087 ends its requested troubleshooting tip at “then:” with no completion.

These are illustrative reviewed cases, not a separately sampled error-rate estimate. All 100 reviews, including stronger cases, are available below.

## Interpretation and limitations

A Human label is detector non-detection of known AI output; it does not establish human authorship or good writing. There is no comparison model, original-text baseline, or human control. This sample cannot estimate detector accuracy, false-positive rate, or improvement over other products. Category samples are only ten each; subcategories have one example. Confidence intervals are descriptive and assume independent observations despite shared model and prompt design. The selected English genres and lengths do not represent all writing.

The quality review covers every completed text; factual verification is targeted rather than exhaustive. See factual_review.md for sources and checked claims. All exact prompts, outputs, labels, and review notes are available in the accompanying HTML and evaluation.json. Raw API responses are retained under write_results.

## Method

100 frozen short prompts; 10 categories; 150, 200, 300, or 500 requested words. First completed output retained, no manual editing or detector feedback. Emulate POST /v1/write, Pangram bulk with explicit pangram-4 selector. Length measured by whitespace-separated tokens including headings; service billing counts may differ.

Returned Pangram versions: {'4.0': 100}. Mean estimated text fractions: {'fraction_ai': 0.10112778827548027, 'fraction_ai_assisted': 0.0, 'fraction_human': 0.8988722121715546}. Fractions describe portions of text, not author-provenance probabilities.

All 100 generation requests succeeded on their first attempt; there were no retries, failed outputs, or score-based selections. Generation ran from 18:18:44 to 18:30:10 UTC on 29 September 2026, with three concurrent requests. Pangram accepted and completed all 100 in one batch. Underlying Emulate model revision and decoding settings were not exposed by the API.

The submitted Pangram payload matches every Emulate output byte-for-byte. Pangram normalizes whitespace in its returned text: all 100 returned texts differ only in whitespace, with no other content mismatch. Raw original and normalized texts are both preserved. Mixed results contain both human- and AI-labeled portions; returned AI-assisted fractions were zero for every document.

An additional returned window-level humanizer flag appeared in 6 documents, all within the Mixed/AI group; it does not change the prespecified primary label mapping. Estimated Pangram bulk cost is $13.76 using whitespace word counts rounded to 100-word blocks and the published $0.04 discounted block rate. This is an estimate, not an invoice; the service's word counting or account terms may differ.
