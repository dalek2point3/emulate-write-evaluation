# Targeted factual review: round 2

These checks support particular review notes. They are not an exhaustive audit of every claim or a corpus-wide accuracy estimate. Fictional scenes are assessed for internal consistency and prompt adherence, not treated as real reporting.

- **W111 (Gandhi): confirmed error.** The text says Gandhi bought Tolstoy Farm. Gandhi's account attributes its purchase to Hermann Kallenbach. Other broad claims about first participation or absence of organizational contact remain unverified here; they should not be treated as established facts. Source: [Gandhi, Ashram Observances in Action, introduction](https://www.mkgandhi.org/ashramobservances/introduction.php).
- **W112 (newspapers): partial check.** The reference to paper-duty repeal in 1861 is consistent with parliamentary debate that year. Parliamentary records also document the 1855 newspaper-stamp legislation. The essay's sweeping description of railway growth from the century's beginning is imprecise; this check does not verify every social-history claim. Sources: [Paper duty debate, 6 May 1861](https://hansard.parliament.uk/commons/1861-05-06/debates/46e4f252-e114-48a6-a5d1-94a0d2c81fd9/WaysAndMeans%E2%80%94PaperDuty), [Newspaper Stamp Act records](https://api.parliament.uk/historic-hansard/acts/newspaper-stamp-act).
- **W114 (Austen): interpretive qualification.** The letter, Lydia/Wickham episode and Darcy's intervention occur in the novel. The output's categorical moral labeling and reduction of Charlotte's decision are interpretive overstatements rather than invented quotations. Source: [Pride and Prejudice, primary text](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/1342/1342-h/1342-h.htm).
- **W121 (urban heat): confirmed conceptual error.** Urban heat islands arise from land cover, materials, urban geometry, and waste heat. The concluding statement that they are caused by global warming incorrectly substitutes a global mechanism for these local mechanisms, despite a more accurate earlier paragraph. Source: [US EPA, What Are Heat Islands?](https://www.epa.gov/heatislands/what-are-heat-islands).
- **W122 (machine learning): largely supported with a qualification.** Training-only preprocessing and avoiding iterative test-set tuning are consistent with official guidance. Adjacent Tuesday/Wednesday observations alone do not establish temporal leakage; the issue is inappropriate availability of future information and/or an evaluation split that does not match deployment. Source: [scikit-learn, Common pitfalls](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/common_pitfalls.html).
- **W123 (vaccination): central mechanisms supported.** Antibodies are proteins; immune memory involves cells, and re-exposure can produce a faster response. The output appropriately avoids universal infection prevention. This is a content check, not individualized vaccination advice. Source: [CDC Pink Book, Principles of Vaccination](https://www.cdc.gov/pinkbook/hcp/table-of-contents/chapter-1-principles-of-vaccination.html).
- **W124 (seasons): core mechanism correct, explanation partly incorrect.** Axial tilt, sunlight angle, and daylight duration explain opposite seasonal patterns. Equal hemisphere-to-Sun distance is not the mechanism of equinox day lengths. The output's exact same-distance assertion is also an overstatement. Source: [NASA, Facts About Earth](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/facts/).
- **W162 (learning): general support, no individual guarantee.** Retrieval practice and spacing have research support; a fixed five-day schedule does not guarantee a particular learner's result. Matching can permit recognition rather than unaided recall. Source: [Dunlosky and Rawson, practice testing and spacing](https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/features/stl-0000024.pdf).

Numerical and logical checks were also made directly against supplied prompt facts (for example, scoring sequences, activity totals, dates, and weights). Review notes distinguish observed contradictions and omissions from claims requiring external verification.
