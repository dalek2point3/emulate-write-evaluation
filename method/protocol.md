# Emulate Write evaluation — prospective protocol

Supersedes the rewrite experiment in docs/protocol.md. The previous AI-authored source texts are unused background artifacts, not inputs or controls in this evaluation.

100 short English prompts across 10 broad categories, each containing 10 distinct subcategories. Each category has two 150-word, three 200-word, three 300-word, and two 500-word requests: 28,000 requested words total. Freeze prompts before service calls. POST /v1/write with prompt and words; retain the first successful response without editing, best-of-N selection, or detector-guided feedback. This tests the product's documented Write pipeline, which itself includes its own rewrite stage.

Generation: keep request, prompt hash, UTC time, raw response, response ID, requested length, returned length, reported charges, and wall latency. Preserve failures. Confirmed uncharged transport/service failures may be retried with full logs; ambiguous timeouts must be reconciled before retry. At most three concurrent requests initially. No humanize endpoint calls.

Detection: submit every completed output, unedited, to Pangram using the verified pangram-4 selector. Save bulk job ID before polling; preserve raw responses and returned model versions. Primary measure is percentage explicitly labeled Human in prediction_short. AI-assisted/mixed are separate and are not fully human passes. Report all exact labels, errors, sample sizes, category rates, word fractions, and descriptive 95% Wilson intervals. No baseline was requested for the revised design. Do not describe results as improvement over another generator or overall detector accuracy. Known provenance is AI regardless of labels.

Writing assessment, performed before looking at detector scores: prompt adherence (1–5), coherence/readability (1–5), specificity/usefulness or imaginative development as appropriate (1–5). 5=fully meets criterion, 4=minor weakness, 3=noticeable weakness, 2=major weakness, 1=fails. These are one assistant reviewer's subjective scores, not a validated benchmark or independent human panel. Retain concrete notes and flag unmet constraints, irrelevant additions, repetition, contradictions, and unsupported factual claims. Record word deviation; use ±20% of requested length as a transparent descriptive tolerance, not a hidden exclusion rule.

Factual review: specifically check substantive factual claims in factual history/science/literature outputs using authoritative sources; distinguish identified errors, verified claims, and unverified claims. Fictional scenarios are not factual reporting. This review is not an exhaustive fact-check of every sentence in all 100 documents. Every completed generation remains in detection results even if it has quality problems.

Limits: one product configuration, one run per prompt, one detector version, English, chosen genres and lengths. Convenience sample, not representative population sampling. No comparison model or authentic-human control; no causal or superiority claims. Per-subcategory n=1 cannot establish category performance; broader categories n=10 remain noisy. Reviewer's knowledge of product identity and prompt affects ratings. Fixed prompts and raw outputs make subsequent independent review possible.
